My pitch — Custom AI Models (angle: agentic loops + end-to-end delivery)

CAI turns messy business data into working AI systems — Data first. Human reviewed. Delivered end to end.

Open promo hub →

I'm Yeriko Vargas, founder of Custom AI Models (CAI). 8.5 years in data science, and I build end-to-end data and AI systems in Python: messy data in, a reviewed, working system out.

For the last 4 years I've been shipping ML, LLM chatbots and agent loops — FastAPI services, MCP tools, and Python orchestration around Claude and the OpenAI API, deployed on Railway and AWS, driven from the terminal.

My roots are statistics, so I care whether the numbers are right before I care whether the demo is pretty. At CAI we start with the data, let the model do the heavy lifting, and keep a human review step in the loop before anything reaches a client.

Right now I'm building past the dashboard: live ecosystem apps — Python + LLM + MCP, with email built in — that act on your data, not just display it.
