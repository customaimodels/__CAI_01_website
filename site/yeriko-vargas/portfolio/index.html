The full story · STAR
S
A live hospitality venue whose music ignored the room: the same playlist for a quiet afternoon and a Friday rush.
T
Pick the next track from the room itself, with no metadata and no listener history.
A
3-minute microphone windows become 100+ spectral, temporal and energy features. A voice-frequency filter estimates crowd activity. The room and the music library live in one feature space, z-scored ((x − mean) / std) and weighted; PCA, KMeans and cosine / Euclidean similarity choose the track, balancing randomness with relevance. R

Vehicle profitability prediction

250,000+ vehicles from Fiat Chrysler. Configuration codes became 108 binary features (engine, trim, interior packages); multilayer perceptrons in R, trained with SGD and binary cross-entropy, classified each configuration above or below $10k, $14k and $17k profit. Benchmarked against reduced multivariate logistic regression, the work narrowed 108 options to 14 drivers. The finding that moved production: about 95% of sales volume came from roughly 11% of configurations.

shape config codes → 108 binary features → MLP vs logistic → 3 profit thresholds → 14 drivers → cut the long tail
