# Custom AI Models (CAI) — Overview

Custom AI Models (CAI) is the solo consultancy of Yeriko Vargas, a senior AI/ML systems engineer.
CAI focuses on designing and shipping production-grade AI systems that plug into a client's existing tools and data.
The work is hands-on: architecture, implementation, and deployment, not just slideware.

CAI is best for teams who:

- Have high-value workflows that are currently manual or brittle.
- Have fragmented data across tools, docs, and systems.
- Care about reliability, latency, and observability — not just demos.
- Want a partner who can reason about both product and infrastructure.

The engagement style is pragmatic and technical:

- Map the workflow and constraints in detail.
- Design the smallest useful system that can own the workflow end-to-end.
- Ship to production quickly, then iterate.

All examples and proof are anonymized. CAI never names confidential clients or venues.
CAI's own price is quoted per engagement and finalized after a short scope call,
with a 50% deposit to start work. Published 2026 market ranges are a sanity check
on any proposal, not a CAI price list.
