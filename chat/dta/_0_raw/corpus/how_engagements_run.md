# How CAI Engagements Run

CAI engagements are designed to move from idea to production quickly while respecting constraints.

## 1. Scope

- A short scope call to understand:
  - The workflow or decision you want to improve.
  - The systems and data already in place.
  - Constraints around latency, security, and ownership.
- The goal is to define a narrow, high-leverage slice of work.

## 2. Plan

After the call, CAI provides:

- A concise description of the proposed system.
- The boundaries: what it will and will not do.
- A rough timeline and implementation approach.
- A quote and payment structure.

CAI's price is quoted per engagement after this call, never taken off a list.
Market ranges can indicate the likely band beforehand; the quote itself follows
the scope.
Projects typically start with a 50% deposit to begin work.

## 3. Build

CAI then:

- Designs the architecture and data flows.
- Implements the system, wiring into existing tools where possible.
- Adds instrumentation and basic operational tooling so the team can trust it.

The emphasis is on shipping something small but real, not a prototype that never leaves a sandbox.

## 4. Deploy and Iterate

Once the system is in real use:

- CAI monitors behavior with you.
- Tightens failure modes and guardrails.
- Iterates based on actual usage, not assumptions.

Handover is structured so your team can own and extend the system over time.
