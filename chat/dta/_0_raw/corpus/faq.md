# FAQ

## How much analyst time does the deal copilot save?

Most teams recover 20–50 hours per deal by reducing document hunting, memo drafting,
and diligence synthesis.

Where the time comes back:

- Questions about deal documents answered in seconds, with sources, instead of hours of searching.
- IC memo sections (risks, business overview, market) drafted from diligence materials, so senior people review instead of write.
- Inconsistencies between commercial, financial, and legal diligence flagged before the IC meeting.

## Can you build X?

Probably, if it fits the philosophy of building production-grade systems around real workflows and data.

If you have a specific idea, briefly describe:

- The workflow you want to improve.
- The tools and data you already use.
- Any hard constraints (compliance, latency, integrations).

The best next step is a short scope call to see if CAI is the right fit.

## How do we get started?

The path is simple:

1. Share a short description of your project and constraints.
2. Do a scope call to map the work and risks.
3. Review a written plan and quote.

If you are ready now, you can send your details in this three-line format:

Name: <your name>
Email: <your email>

## What does it cost?

CAI does not publish a fixed price list. What a project costs is quoted after a
scope call, and CAI charges per project rather than per hour.

What the market charges in 2026, read across six published pricing guides:

- Simple AI chatbot: $10,000 to $30,000.
- Custom RAG and business chatbot: $30,000 to $70,000.
- Advanced AI system with integrations and support: $60,000 to $150,000+.

Those are market ranges, useful for sanity-checking any proposal. They are not a
CAI price list, and CAI does not quote against them without knowing the scope.

What decides the band is how much of the business the system has to understand and
safely act on, not which AI model is behind it. Answering questions sits low.
Answering from your own documents, prices and policies sits in the middle. Taking
actions and connecting to live systems sits at the top.

Budget for the running cost as well: maintenance runs 15 to 25 percent of the
build per year across this market.

How CAI structures payment on a project:

- A 50% deposit to begin work.
- The remainder on delivery milestones.

## Do you work with small teams or only large companies?

Both. What matters is:

- The importance of the workflow or decision you are improving.
- Whether there is a clear owner on your side.
- Whether we can draw a straight line from the system to business value.

## What models and tools do you use?

CAI is model- and vendor-agnostic.

The focus is on:

- Choosing models that fit the problem and constraints.
- Designing the surrounding system so it is observable, debuggable, and changeable.
- Avoiding unnecessary complexity.

The exact tools and models are chosen per engagement and can evolve over time.
